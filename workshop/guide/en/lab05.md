# Lab 5 — Softmax, temperature, and text generation

*Objective: turn scores into probabilities and generate text, one token at a time.*

### Why
Logits are unnormalized scores. **Softmax** turns them into **probabilities**: all positive and adding up to 1.

**Temperature** adjusts how adventurous the model is: logits are divided by it before softmax.
- Low temperature (less than 1): the favorite becomes even more likely, and the text becomes repetitive.
- High temperature (greater than 1): the chances become more similar, and the text becomes more unpredictable.

Generating text means repeating three steps: calculate the next token's probabilities, **sample** a token, and append it to the end. Then do it all again.

### Predict
What happens to the probabilities as the temperature approaches 0?
@answer Almost all the probability goes to the highest-scoring token. In the limit, the model always chooses the favorite.

### Copy and paste

- `logits[:, -1, :]` takes only the last position: this is the one that predicts the next token.
